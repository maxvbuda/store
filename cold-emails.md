## Sending notes

- **Plain text.** No images, no tracking pixel, no logo. HTML mail from an
  unknown sender is the single strongest spam signal you control.
- **Separate domain.** Send from `getoperator.co`, not your main domain. If it
  gets burned, your real mail keeps working. Warm it for 2–3 weeks first.
- **Under ~50/day per inbox** until the domain has history.
- **One link, or none.** Emails 4 and 2 ask them to reply for the link — that
  beats a link in a first touch.
- **Lowercase subjects, no emoji, no "Re:" fakery.** A false `Re:` is a
  deceptive subject line and it is exactly what CAN-SPAM prohibits.

**Legal, briefly and accurately:** US CAN-SPAM requires a real physical postal
address in the message, a working opt-out, and honouring opt-outs within 10
business days. It applies to B2B cold email — there is no business-to-business
exemption. If you're mailing UK or EU recipients, GDPR/PECR is stricter; B2B
legitimate interest can cover it, but corporate-sounding addresses like
`hello@` are safer than named individuals. Add a one-line footer to every one of
these:

> Operator, [street address]. Don't want these? Reply "stop" and I'll remove you.

## One honest flag before you send these

These emails claim the agent **runs** marketing, sales and customer service on a
real Shopify store. What is verified working today is the machinery: the agent
holds a goal, drives a real logged-in browser, reads pages, acts, and hands back
when it hits a wall — proven end to end on a live run.

What has **not** been proven yet is a full unattended shift on a real store —
answering a day of real tickets, or running a drip to completion, without a
person watching. Until you've done that once yourself, emails 1, 2, 3 and 6 are
promising slightly more than you've seen work.

Email 5 is the one that is fully true today, which is why I'd lead with it if
you want to start sending this week. Otherwise: run the agent against your own
store for a few days first, then send 1 or 3 — they'll be true, and you'll have
a real story for the reply.
